# Style guide
English; formal, direct, scientific-question-driven. Use a technical-challenge introduction: compression reduces text but may leave visual premises unsupported. Distinguish literature observations from planned work. No unverified first claims, causal rewriting claims, marketing language, or invented results. Use numbered citations with linked arXiv records; use preprint years consistently rather than mixing venue years. Preserve canonical terminology and notation. Pipeline is a TikZ schematic of proposed operations, not empirical evidence; labels must remain legible at final size.
